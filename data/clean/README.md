# Clean data

Generated by `python build_clean_data.py` from the raw NBA.com cache in `data/raw/` (regular season only). Do not edit by hand.

## Files

### `league_seasons.csv`

League averages per team-game, one row per season since 1979-80. 47 rows.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `season` | Starting year (2025 = 2025-26) |
| `season_label` | e.g. 2025-26 |
| `team_games` | Team-games played league-wide |
| `ppg` | Points per team-game |
| `fg3m_pg` | 3-pointers made per team-game (from 1979-80) |
| `fg3a_pg` | 3-point attempts per team-game (from 1985-86) |
| `fg3a_rate` | Share of field-goal attempts that were threes (from 1985-86) |
| `fg3_pct` | 3-point percentage (from 1985-86) |
| `efg` | Effective FG%: (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) / FGA |
| `pace` | Possessions per 48 min (from 1985-86) |
| `ortg` | Points per 100 possessions (from 1985-86) |
| `shortened` | Why the season was short (lockout / COVID), else blank |

### `team_seasons.csv`

One row per team per season since 1985-86. 1,177 rows.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `team_id` | NBA.com franchise id (stable across moves and renames) |
| `team` | Abbreviation used that season |
| `team_name` | Name used that season |
| `games / wins / win_pct` | Regular-season record |
| `fg3a_pg / fg3a_rate / fg3_pct / efg` | Shooting volume and accuracy (see league_seasons) |
| `ortg / drtg / net_rtg` | Points scored / allowed per 100 possessions, and the difference |
| `*_z` | Same stat as a z-score within its season (0 = that season's league average) |

### `win_correlations.csv`

Pearson r between win % and each shooting metric, per season, per era, and pooled. 138 rows.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `period / first_season / last_season` | Season range the row covers |
| `metric` | fg3a_rate (volume), fg3_pct (accuracy) or efg (overall efficiency) |
| `r` | Correlation with win % (multi-season periods use within-season z-scores) |
| `n` | Team-seasons in the period |

### `quadrants.csv`

Average win % by above/below-average 3PA rate × 3P%, per era. 20 rows.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `volume / accuracy` | high = above that season's league average |
| `win_pct` | Mean win % |
| `n` | Team-seasons in the group |

### `zone_seasons.csv`

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `zone` | NBA.com zone name (Corner 3 = left + right corner) |

| `share` | Share of the season's attempts |

### `shot_grid.csv`

Every field-goal attempt binned into 1 ft × 1 ft half-court cells by shot zone, per season (the website samples shot particles from it). 58,290 rows.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `x / y` | Lower-left corner of the cell in feet from the hoop (x: left/right, y: towards half court) |
| `zone` | RA = restricted area, PAINT = paint outside it, MID = mid-range, C3 = corner three, AB3 = above-the-break three |
| `fga / fgm` | Attempts and makes from the cell in that zone |

## Cleaning rules

1. A season-level stat is kept only when at least 99% of that season's games report every box-score column it needs. NBA.com leaves FGA blank before 1981-82 and 3PA, turnovers and rebounds blank for most games before 1985-86, so 3PA-based stats, pace and ratings start in 1985-86 and 3PM in 1979-80.
2. Teams are joined on NBA.com's franchise id, so relocations and renames (Seattle → Oklahoma City, New Jersey → Brooklyn, Charlotte/New Orleans) stay one franchise; labels use that season's abbreviation.
3. Games listed with no result are removed (one cancelled game: Indiana at Boston, 2013-04-16, stored as two 0-point rows), so Boston and Indiana have 81 games in 2012-13.
4. Lockout and COVID seasons (1998-99, 2011-12, 2019-20, 2020-21) are kept but flagged; every comparison uses per-game rates or win %, never season totals.
5. To compare teams across eras, shooting stats are also given as z-scores within their own season.
6. Shot charts: team files for franchises that did not exist that season are empty and skipped; heaves from beyond half court are dropped from the grid but counted when checking totals.
7. Shot zones are rebuilt from the shot charts and must agree with the shot type. NBA.com draws its zones with today's 23.75 ft arc, so in 1996-97, when the line was 22 ft all around, 19,165 threes (a tenth of all shots) are labeled Mid-Range. That inflated the season's mid-range share from 29.8% to 40.0%. Threes outside a 3-point zone are moved to the corner or above-the-break zone by location, and twos inside one to Mid-Range. Left and right corners are combined; backcourt heaves are excluded.
8. Players: traded players have one combined row under their last team. The radar only ranks players with 500+ minutes and 20+ games, against the same season's players.
