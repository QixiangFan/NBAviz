    Final buzzer

    Making beats taking

    How closely each stat tracks winning, 2025-26 (correlation with win %)

    1. The three-point revolution is real. Attempts per team-game rose about elevenfold since 1985-86, and the mid-range share of shots fell from 37% in 1997-98 to 10% in 2025-26.
    2. Taking more threes is no longer linked to winning more. Volume’s link to winning faded once every team shot threes. Accuracy and overall efficiency track win % far more closely, and accuracy’s link keeps rising.
    3. Next: test the pattern on playoff teams and champions, and follow the players who take the shots.

    Limits

    • Regular season only. With 23–30 teams a season, single-season correlations are noisy.
    • Correlation, not causation. Rule changes appear because they happened alongside the trend.
    • 3-point attempts were not logged for every game before 1985-86. Shot locations start in 1996-97.

    Data & methods

    NBA.com stats through nba_api, cleaned by build_clean_data.py. The clean tables, data dictionary, cleaning rules and validation checks are in data/clean/. Correlations compare each team only with its own season’s league (z-scores). Player photos and current logos: NBA.com. Older logos: SportsLogos.net.
